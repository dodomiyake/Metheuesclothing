## 5. Cancelling — your 14-day right

service, we refund the standard rate, not the premium.

thing.

Goods must come back in a resaleable condition. You may handle them as you
would in a shop; if you reduce their value by doing more than that, we may
reduce your refund accordingly.

  that is not possible you may have a refund.
- **After six months**, the same rights apply but you may be asked to show the
  fault was there at delivery.

Nothing in these terms limits these rights.
